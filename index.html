HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag left or right anywhere on the screen to bend the path ahead of you. You never move the dot directly.
  • Once you pass a stretch of path it freezes solid. The pale tip behind you is still safe; everything past the white marker will kill you.
  • The arena curls, so you come back around into the line you just drew. Old path ages from cyan to red, then burns away.
  • Shave past your line, the wall or a hazard for a near miss. Chain them for a combo multiplier.
  • Grab a green core to purge the old path around it and buy yourself room.
